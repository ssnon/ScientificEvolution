## 1. Central question and what counts as progress

> Can a self-directed population of **fallible** research agents turn actual evidence, quantitative limits, strong competing explanations, mathematical criticism and independent peer insights into **an experiment-worthy, independently reviewable pre-experimental hypothesis** — or rationally conclude that the proposed hypotheses are not yet test-worthy under stated conditions?

The immediate scientific endpoint is **before new lab validation**. A convincing case need not prove which SERS mechanism occurs. It must make a concrete proposed experiment intellectually worth running: which distinctive observables/interventions could separate a proposed mechanism from serious admissible alternatives; in which physically plausible parameter regime; what observations would falsify or fail to distinguish it; and which prior claims remain uncertain. A rigorous scoped negative, demonstrated indistinguishability boundary or justified scientific HOLD is substantive progress **but not automatically a positive Level II candidate**.

Individual components **are not required to succeed**. Missing full text, a bad math review, an invalid numeric request, a negative result, a rejected hypothesis, an unselected PEER idea or a changed research direction can all be legitimate outcomes. The systemic failure to avoid is *epistemic misrepresentation*: reporting a task as evidence completed when only a different theory was produced, promoting bibliographic metadata to full-text observations, forgetting an important correction, or claiming peer synthesis merely from imports or parent counts. Agent failures should be recoverable or preserved as open uncertainty without an artificial success quota.

## 3. Target scientific evaluation (post-hoc, not runtime quotas)

For the strongest **AI-selected** candidate(s), or for a meaningful negative/defer, independently read the actual saved scientific case and ask:

1. **Distinctive claim.** What precisely differs from known charge-gating, orientation/adsorption, hotspot, field/Stark, thermal, photochemical, detector and hidden-state hypotheses? Is the novelty claim bounded to what was actually inspected, without inventing prior-art absence?
2. **Physical / mathematical credibility.** Which assumptions, units, measurement operators, rates and feasible parameter ranges support the effect? Are small-parameter approximations, finite exposure and steady-state/transient distinctions respected? Which portions are actual tool-backed derivations/computations versus model-authored mathematics?
3. **Strong null / identifiability.** Can at least the relevant strongest *admissible* rival models reproduce the proposed discriminating observations under comparable measurement/noise/nuisance constraints? If so, is this a scoped negative or can justified new observations/interventions separate them? Do not demand proof against every imaginable null.
4. **Experiment proposal.** What can a competent independent laboratory actually vary and observe; at what time/energy/spectral resolution; which positive, negative, null and confound controls; what result would genuinely falsify the claim? Are promised controls physically practical given known information?
5. **Evidence honesty and research response.** Did the chosen Work perform an actual source inspection/calculation? If it failed, did the next research decision recognize the limitation and either retry by a defensible method, pivot, or defer? Is the best proposed candidate being supported by the *right* evidence class?
6. **Cumulative collective reasoning.** Do reviewer corrections, negative results, independent peer challenges and earlier-generation findings demonstrably change a subsequent claim, research decision or scope? Require substantive comparison where applicable, not particular parent links.

A defensible Level II outcome is **a human-review-ready pre-experimental scientific case**, still labeled proposed/unverified, with supportable novelty scope, quantitative basis, strong-null and falsification discussion. A partial candidate, scoped negative, evidence-limited HOLD or productive pivot must be reported as exactly that; they are not forced into a Level II success story.

## 6. Decision points after the integrated evaluation

- **Defensible pre-experimental candidate:** retain source and assumptions, label explicitly `CANDIDATE_FOR_INDEPENDENT_REVIEW`, obtain independent scientific scrutiny. This is not experimental validation or guaranteed novelty.
- **Defensible scoped negative / non-identifiability / HOLD:** preserve it as a valid scientific result and rationale; research may autonomously branch, pivot or stop. Do not pretend a positive candidate was achieved.
- **Meaningful science but incomplete case:** report exactly which scientific uncertainties remain and whether the scientist addressed them responsibly. A Worker that did not retrieve a paper or a reviewer that initially erred does **not** automatically imply system failure.
- **Repeated unsupported completion/false evidence:** prioritize a general-purpose observation/feedback deficiency (if demonstrably caused by implementation) or explicitly identify a model-reasoning limitation, then decide whether further development is justified. Do not add more checks without a causal diagnosis.
- **Repeated claim proliferation without increased discriminatory content:** treat as absence of demonstrated scientific convergence, not a reason to require arbitrary parent links or mandatory hypotheses.
