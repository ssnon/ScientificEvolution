## What counts as a scientific advance

A research step is scientifically substantive when there is a **traceable causal lineage** from an existing hypothesis, null, source or calculation to a genuinely changed scientific claim. Record the original and new IDs, differences in mechanism/assumptions/constraints/predictions, competing null explanations, exact literature evidence class (metadata vs inspected body), executed calculation and its limitations, expected distinguishing measurement, and reasons to continue, fork, reject or pause.

Record separately: `IMPLEMENTED` (code exists); `MOCK_VERIFIED` (controlled fixture); `LIVE_EXECUTION_OBSERVED` (actual saved external model, real tool result or Journal event observed in user-provided logs); `SCIENTIFICALLY_REVIEWED` (substantive critical review; specify who/what); `LEVEL_II_CANDIDATE` (review-worthy conditional proposal, not experimental validation). **Do not infer higher levels from lower ones.**
