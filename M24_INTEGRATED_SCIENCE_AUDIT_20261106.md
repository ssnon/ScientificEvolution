## Executive scientific judgment

M24 achieved a continuous 2-generation autonomous operational run, meaningful model-mediated mathematical corrections and two bounded examples of real numerical work that changed conditional scientific assertions. However, repeated selection of primary-study audits did not result in newly read primary-study body text; the new PEER discoveries were not demonstrably synthesized in Population or developed directly in the next generation. No *independently reviewable complete pre-experimental Level II case* has been established on the available material. This is **partial scientific progress**, not either a blanket failure or a Level II success.

**Correct target:** Level II is a test-worthy scientific *proposal before new experiments*. It requires a distinctive claim, defended physical/quantitative assumptions, serious null comparisons and their identifiability boundaries, appropriately supported prior-art status, and a feasible falsifying test or measurement plan. A measured real-world SERS mechanism or newly collected empirical data is *not* a precondition of reaching Level II. Equally, a model-authored prediction alone does not meet all Level II requirements.
