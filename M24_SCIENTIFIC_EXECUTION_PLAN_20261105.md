# M24 — Research Execution, Evidence Truth and Scientific Synthesis

**Proposed:** 2026-11-05 (KST)  
**Status:** next development/research plan, *not yet implemented*.  
**Mission:** general-purpose autonomous AI Scientist; SERS is the current testbed, not a hard-coded hypothesis or permanent product domain.

## 1. Why this milestone, not another math-verifier revision

The 2026-11-04 ASER/C04 program was motivated by an undetected stationary-harmonic finite-exposure formula error. Claim-level independent mathematical verification in V1.1/V1.2 subsequently **found and corrected several genuine errors**, including a two-state CTMC dwell/occupancy inversion and a windowed covariance nonoverlap factor. In V1.2, incomplete peer verification was truthfully handed off without blocking Population. This is a useful but *limited* mathematical self-correction capability; unselected premises, reviewer failures, and actual tool-backed mathematical verification remain open questions. There is no evidence that the exact original C04 premise has been autonomously rederived and corrected in its own context.

The **remaining high-value bottleneck** was visible before math verification: a scientifically sensible selected task does not consistently produce a properly executed/inspected and interpreted research result. In V1.2 Population, Controller D00001–D00003 correctly treated literature absence as unproven and preferred primary-source inspection to additional speculative mechanism generation. The selected FRESH primary audit returned `EVIDENCE_UNAVAILABLE` while publishing new theoretical charge-gating assets; much subsequent work was mathematical review. Another modular audit was still active. The eight new live assets did not have explicit peer-asset parents despite 15 imported PEER assets. These observations **do not** justify a compulsory science policy or an assertion that peer comparisons never occurred.

## 2. Main research/development question

> Can the AI Scientist translate a scientifically autonomous choice into an actual evidence- or computation-bearing Work, interpret success and failure without inventing a result, and then make a traceable subsequent scientific decision, while retaining mathematics correction and independent PEER alternatives?

An appropriate answer may be a verified source observation, an executed computation with physically meaningful assumptions, a valid counterexample, a scoped nonidentifiability result, a revised hypothesis, or a reasoned no-evidence/unavailable/defer decision. A forced positive Level II outcome is **not** an acceptable measure of success.

Track only the causal path:

`Controller selection -> Worker request -> real tool action/outcome -> Worker interpretation -> registered claim/limitation -> next Controller rationale`.

Classify the concrete gap as **(a)** inaccessible/nonexistent primary material, **(b)** missing/broken tool capability or request-contract issue, **(c)** a successful retrieval obscured by feedback/representation, **(d)** Worker task drift or unjustified theorizing, **(e)** mathematically valid but scientifically uninformative computation, or **(f)** simply incomplete research at operational boundary. Multiple classifications may apply. Do **not** assume the cause before inspecting the wire/tool results.

Deliverable: one concise, source-linked root-cause note with raw call/tool identifiers and scientific consequence. No quota, generic policy, or extra tests unless evidence identifies a defect.

## 4. Engineering change *only if warranted*

If the selected Work failed because of a concrete **general-purpose** tooling or feedback defect, change the responsible Native code path rather than stacking another root-specific shim:

- Make real tool outcomes and primary-source content/availability distinguishable from bibliographic discovery, model text, `plan_only`, and process-level `EXECUTED`. Preserve the complete original status/source chain.
- Expose enough task outcome to Worker/Controller to let them decide whether a result answers the question, which missing evidence remains, whether an alternative source is useful, and whether to defer. Do not auto-synthesize a plausible result from metadata or retry an uncertain billed model call.
- If the Work returns a model-authored new hypothesis rather than the evidence it was tasked to inspect, preserve that hypothesis as *model theory*, not as a successful literature audit. Feedback may show task/outcome mismatch, but it must not rewrite scientific content or force a particular research conclusion.
- Preserve FRESH independence, P0 and Journal truth, exact root-scoped Source/Parent permissions, historical Work non-executability, and the functioning math-verification/uncertainty handoff.

Only focused regression cases that demonstrate the specific defect are necessary. Freeze the new Python source, create a new Source-Pinned research root, and never patch the old V1.1/V1.2 Journal or source pin in place. Prefer one integrated end-to-end study to a proliferation of small paid probes.

## 5. Autonomous scientific continuation

Give the next Population a **genuine opportunity** to carry out its own chosen high-value tasks from the inherited portfolio. If a new generation is required, import the V1.2 G0 Population as historical state; old `ACTIVE` Works remain historical and are **not** magically resumed. The AI may voluntarily create a new Work to continue one, select a different problem, or scientifically defer. Any new FRESH Work must not be exposed to inherited hypotheses.

In the resulting study assess, without making them fixed runtime requirements:

1. **Chosen scientific priority:** did model-authored reasoning identify an information-bearing next action, with explicit uncertainty about prior art, feasible controls, and strong nulls when appropriate?
2. **Actual research action:** was an authorized primary source read or actual calculation/experiment executed and returned? If unavailable, is the limitation accurately represented and followed by a scientifically meaningful choice?
3. **Scientific delta:** did evidence/derivation/negative result change an assumption, quantitative bound, predictive contrast, error judgment, null comparison or research priority? Repetition and extra assets are not themselves progress.
4. **Cross-PEER synthesis:** was inherited peer critique or the order/polarity intervention proposal explicitly compared, adopted, rejected, or otherwise used, *if scientifically relevant*? Record context availability separately from explicit consideration and parent-linked descendants. Do not mandate use of SYN_02 or any other asset.
5. **Mathematical integrity:** if mathematical claims arise, did independent criticism detect relevant flaws and did subsequent scientific reasoning respect corrections? Distinguish model rederivation from actually executed numerical/symbolic verification.
6. **Honest conclusion:** Level II requires defensible distinct causal/predictive content, physically meaningful constraints, discriminators against strong admissible nulls, relevant prior art and falsification route suitable for independent review. `ONGOING`, scoped negative or HOLD may be valid outcomes.

## 6. Anti-goals and scope boundaries

- Do not enforce arbitrary recursion depth, verification-work quotas, fixed Controller-versus-Worker proportions, mandatory peer-parent links, predetermined mechanisms, or a required positive finding.
- Do not mistake `COMPLETE` (operational cycle recorded) for P0 resolution, a saved API response for executed science, an access failure for nonexistence of literature, or mathematically correct correlations for causal identifiability.
- Do not prioritize token/cost trimming by default. Call and spend limits, if used, are transparent *operational* constraints only.
- Do not introduce a new source-format/recovery wrapper unless the observed run actually fails at that boundary.
- Do not hide corrections: errors and revised claims should coexist in durable provenance.
